Mail Peek

100% faithful .msg / .eml email preview

Click an email attachment to read it in a popup. Hold Shift while clicking to download the original file. Zoom with Ctrl/⌘ + / −. The Reply / Reply all / Forward buttons open the email in your default mail app (the app associated with .msg / .eml files).
